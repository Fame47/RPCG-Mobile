V27

- Cleaned the hammer-to-full-logo handoff so the base/full-logo no longer overlap after the shake.

- Every Area now generates a randomized guaranteed route with exactly:
  * 4 normal battle nodes
  * 2 Camp-or-Treasure choice nodes
  * 1 Elite node
  * 1 random Cave or Dungeon node
  * 1 Area Boss
- First choice is always after the first two normal fights.
- Second choice appears only after three more combat nodes.
- Elite always occurs after the first choice.
- Cave/Dungeon always occurs after the Elite.

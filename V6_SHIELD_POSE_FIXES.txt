RPCG MOBILE ADVENTURE - KNIGHT MOVES V6

Shield pose lifecycle fixes:
- Shield Block now holds only through the enemy turn and returns to the normal Knight pose at the START of the player's next turn.
- Sword & Shield uses the same lifecycle: combo3 -> Shield Block hold -> normal pose at the start of the next player turn.
- Ending the turn early no longer allows the Shield Block artwork to persist into the next player turn.
- Knight Shield Up now switches to the Shield Block pose when the Knight's turn ends and holds it for the full enemy turn.
- Shield Up pose clears at the start of the player's next turn.
- Enemy-turn safeguard ensures Shield Up still displays the block pose even when the Knight ended the turn through Study, a skipped phase, or another alternate turn-end path.
- Existing Shield Block impact shake remains active while the pose is held.
